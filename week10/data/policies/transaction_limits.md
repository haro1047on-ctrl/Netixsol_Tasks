# Zarya Digital Bank — Transaction & Account Limits
**Compliance Standard:** SBP Branchless & Digital Banking Framework 2022  
**Applicability:** All Tier 0 and Tier 1 Retail Accounts  

---

## 1. Asaan Digital Account (Level 0 — Basic CNIC Verified)
- **Eligibility:** Pakistani citizens with valid NADRA CNIC verified via mobile OTP.
- **Daily Debit Limit:** PKR 200,000.
- **Monthly Debit Limit:** PKR 1,000,000.
- **Maximum Total Credit Balance:** PKR 1,000,000 at any given time.
- **Account Upgrade:** Can be upgraded to Level 1 at zero cost via in-app biometric verification.

---

## 2. Fully Biometric Verified Digital Account (Level 1 — NADRA Biometric)
- **Eligibility:** Live facial/fingerprint biometric verification via Zarya App or partner merchant.
- **Daily Debit Limit:** PKR 1,000,000.
- **Monthly Debit Limit:** PKR 5,000,000.
- **Maximum Total Credit Balance:** Uncapped (No balance ceiling).
- **International Inward Remittances:** Uncapped via Roshan Digital / Home Remittance channels.

---

## 3. Raast Instant Payment Limits
- **Raast P2P Single Transaction Limit:** PKR 200,000.
- **Raast P2P Daily Cumulative Limit:** PKR 500,000.
- **Raast P2M (Merchant Payment) Daily Limit:** PKR 1,000,000.
- **Raast Alias:** 1 active mobile number can be linked to 1 Raast IBAN. Aliases can be changed instantly in app.

---

## 4. ATM Cash Withdrawal Daily Limits
- **PayPak Classic Debit Card:** PKR 50,000 per calendar day.
- **PayPak Gold Debit Card:** PKR 100,000 per calendar day.
- **Visa International Debit Card:** PKR 150,000 per calendar day.
- **POS / E-commerce Daily Spend Limit:** Up to PKR 250,000 (configurable in app card security settings).
