# Zarya Digital Bank — Schedule of Charges (SOC)
**Effective Period:** January 1, 2026 – June 30, 2026  
**Regulatory Authority:** State Bank of Pakistan (SBP)  
**Applicability:** Retail Digital Banking Customers  

---

## 1. Raast & Digital Fund Transfers
- **Raast P2P (Person-to-Person) Instant Transfers:** Completely FREE (PKR 0.00). Mandated by SBP Digital Payments Directive.
- **Raast P2M (Person-to-Merchant) QR Payments:** FREE for retail buyers.
- **1Link Inter-Bank Funds Transfer (IBFT):**
  - First PKR 25,000 cumulative monthly transfers: FREE (PKR 0.00).
  - Subsequent transfers exceeding PKR 25,000: 0.10% of transaction amount or PKR 200 (whichever is lower), plus applicable provincial sales tax.
- **Zarya-to-Zarya Internal Account Transfers:** Unlimited and completely FREE.

---

## 2. Debit Card Issuance & Annual Charges
- **PayPak Classic Debit Card:**
  - Annual Subscription Fee: PKR 1,200 + FED/sales tax.
  - Card Replacement / Re-issuance Fee: PKR 800 + tax.
- **PayPak Gold Debit Card:**
  - Annual Subscription Fee: PKR 1,800 + FED/sales tax.
  - Card Replacement / Re-issuance Fee: PKR 1,000 + tax.
- **Visa International Debit Card:**
  - Annual Subscription Fee: PKR 2,400 + FED/sales tax.
  - International Debit Card Replacement Fee: PKR 1,000 + tax.
  - Cross-Border / Foreign Currency Transaction Fee: 3.5% over SBP interbank rate.

---

## 3. ATM Transaction Charges
- **Cash Withdrawal at Zarya Digital Bank ATMs:** FREE for all Zarya cardholders.
- **Cash Withdrawal at Other 1Link Switch ATMs:** PKR 23.44 per transaction (includes 1Link switch fee and Federal Excise Duty).
- **ATM Balance Inquiry at Other 1Link ATMs:** PKR 5.00 inclusive of tax.
- **ATM Mini-Statement Generation (1Link ATMs):** PKR 5.00 per inquiry.

---

## 4. SMS Alerts & Digital Services
- **Mandatory Digital Banking Security SMS Alerts:**
  - Per Month Subscription Fee: PKR 150 + applicable tax.
  - Critical OTP, Login Alerts, and Transaction Notification SMS: FREE of charge.
- **E-Statements (Delivered via Email / Mobile App):** Completely FREE.
- **Paper Statement of Account (Branch counter printout):** PKR 100 per copy.
