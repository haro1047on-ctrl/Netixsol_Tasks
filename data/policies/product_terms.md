# Zarya Digital Bank — Retail Product Terms & Conditions
**Banking License:** SBP Digital Retail Bank (DRB) License 2022  
**Governing Law:** Banking Companies Ordinance 1962 & SBP Guidelines  

---

## 1. Zarya Digital Current Account
- **Minimum Initial Deposit:** PKR 0.00 (Zero balance onboarding).
- **Minimum Balance Maintenance Requirement:** None. No penalty or service charge for low balances.
- **Account Opening Channel:** 100% digital onboarding via Zarya Mobile App in under 3 minutes.
- **Cheque Book Facility:** First 25-leaf cheque book free upon customer biometric verification; subsequent books PKR 25 per leaf.

---

## 2. Zarya Asaan Savings Account
- **Profit Calculation:** Calculated daily on closing balance and credited on the 1st of every calendar month.
- **Indicative Expected Profit Rate:** 18.50% per annum (subject to SBP Monetary Policy rate revisions).
- **Tax Withholding on Profit:**
  - Active Taxpayers (FBR Filer): 15% withholding tax deducted at source.
  - Non-Active Taxpayers (FBR Non-Filer): 30% withholding tax deducted at source.
- **Zakat Deduction:** Applicable on 1st Ramadan under Zakat and Ushr Ordinance 1980 unless statutory CZ-50 exemption affidavit is submitted before 1st Shaban.

---

## 3. Zarya Nano Salary Micro-Loan
- **Facility Type:** Unsecured revolving short-term digital credit.
- **Financing Amount:** Minimum PKR 10,000; Maximum PKR 50,000.
- **Repayment Tenure:** 30, 60, or 90 days.
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  - Pakistani citizen aged 21 to 55 years.
  - Minimum 3 consecutive monthly salary credits into Zarya Digital Bank account.
  - Satisfactory credit history with zero active defaults on SBP Electronic Credit Information Bureau (e-CIB).
- **Markup Rate:** 2.2% flat per month on outstanding principal.

## 4. Complimentary Takaful & Insurance Protection
- **PayPak Gold & Visa Debit Cardholders:**
  - Complimentary accidental death and permanent disability Takaful cover up to PKR 500,000.
  - Active condition: Minimum 1 retail POS or e-commerce transaction in the preceding 30 days.
