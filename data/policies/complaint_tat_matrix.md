# Zarya Digital Bank — Complaint Turnaround Time (TAT) Matrix
**Regulatory Framework:** State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) Consumer Protection Regulations (BC & CPD)  
**Dispute Escalation Authority:** SBP Sunwai Portal  

---

## 1. ATM Dispense Failures & Retractions (Mandatory SBP SLA)
- **Applicable Circular:** SBP BPRD Circular 01 of 2020.
- **Scenario:** Customer balance debited at 1Link ATM, but physical cash is not dispensed or is retracted.
- **Resolution Turnaround Time (TAT):** Maximum **48 business hours** from initial complaint logging.
- **Remediation Procedure:**
  1. Electronic Journal (EJ) log fetched from acquiring bank via 1Link switch.
  2. If cash overage confirmed, funds auto-credited directly to customer balance.
  3. SMS notification dispatched with transaction reference number.

---

## 2. Raast & 1Link Inter-Bank Fund Transfer (IBFT) Disputes
- **Scenario:** Funds deducted from sender's account but beneficiary not credited.
- **Initial Acknowledgment:** Immediate issuance of 12-digit Retrieval Reference Number (RRN).
- **Inter-Bank Switch Reconciliation:** **24 to 48 business hours**.
- **Maximum Resolution SLA:** **7 business days** under 1Link Dispute Arbitration Rules.
- **Customer Advice:** Recheck beneficiary account after 24 hours; if uncredited, reverse debit is executed.

---

## 3. Disputed POS / E-Commerce Card Transactions
- **Scenario:** Unauthorized merchant charge or double debit on Visa / PayPak debit cards.
- **Chargeback Filing Deadline:** Customer must report dispute within 30 days of transaction date.
- **Resolution Turnaround Time (TAT):**
  - PayPak Domestic Disputes: Up to **15 business days**.
  - Visa International Disputes: Up to **30 to 45 business days** subject to Visa International Arbitration.

---

## 4. Fraud & Account Compromise Protocol
- **Action Timeline:** Immediate temporary freeze on cards and digital banking channels within **15 minutes** of report.
- **Helpline Assistance:** 24/7 dedicated Fraud Prevention Unit helpline (021-111-927-927).
- **Internal Investigation TAT:** 14 business days.

---

## 5. Escalation to State Bank of Pakistan (SBP Sunwai)
- **Eligibility:** If a complaint remains unresolved beyond mandated bank TAT or customer is dissatisfied with resolution.
- **SBP Sunwai Portal URL:** `https://sunwai.sbp.org.pk`
- **SBP Helpline:** `111-727-273` (111-SBP-BPR)
- **Banking Mohtasib Pakistan:** Complaints can also be escalated to Banking Mohtasib (`https://www.bankingmohtasib.gov.pk`).
